## One financial picture. Coordinated decisions.

Financial lives are connected, but most financial tools are not. Tax choices affect investments. Debt affects cash flow. Insurance and estate decisions shape long term wealth. When each area is managed separately, important opportunities can be missed.

Alpheva continuously analyzes the member's complete financial picture, identifies opportunities across financial domains, explains why they matter, and helps turn insight into action.

## How it works

1. **Connect everything:** Link financial institutions through Plaid and securely add relevant financial documents.
2. **Build context:** Create a unified view of the household's accounts, goals, financial activity, and planning information.
3. **Find opportunities:** Apply document intelligence and complex financial reasoning across the complete picture.
4. **Act and measure:** Prioritize recommendations, support execution, and track financial progress over time.

## Security and trust

Protecting financial information is foundational to Alpheva.

- Secure financial institution connectivity through **Plaid**
- **Stripe** payment processing, with card information tokenized and handled outside Alpheva's servers
- **AES 256** encryption for data at rest and **TLS 1.2+** for network communication
- Least privilege access controls, multifactor authentication, and secure IAM practices
- Continuous monitoring, logging, alerting, and secure development practices
- Secure, reliable, and scalable infrastructure built on **AWS**
- Privacy practices aligned with **CCPA and CPRA**

Read more in the [Alpheva Trust & Security Center](https://alpheva.com/trust-and-security/).
